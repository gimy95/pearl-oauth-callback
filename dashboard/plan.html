1 · What the data tells us

✅ What works

  • 10–12k months were the best months. July and August booked at about 130 SAR per booking. More spend in September bought fewer bookings.
  • TikTok Male-All-Laser is the workhorse: about 70% of TikTok lead spend at ~26 CPL.
  • Women's 196–199 SAR laser offers book at about 170–210 per booking on Snapchat. "Women 4 areas 199" is the best current Snapchat creative.
  • Post-style ads (TikTok Female TiktokPosts, Instagram IG Posts) give the cheapest leads. Judge them by CPB, not CPL.
  • Leads that reach WhatsApp book 4× more often (20% vs 4.5% on TikTok). Fast follow-up matters as much as the ad.

❌ Where money was wasted

  • TikTok video views: 2,840 SAR in Sep–Oct for zero leads.
  • TikTok Hydrafacial ad group: 1,508 SAR for 13 leads.
  • Snapchat Male Summer 224: 5,723 SAR at 358 per booking.
  • Snapchat 8 Oct refresh: five of six new ads lost, about 2,570 SAR for 4 bookings.
  • Outages: ads off 5–10 Oct; TikTok near zero from 22 Oct; 1–2 Nov almost no delivery.
  • Meta in June: 54 cheap leads, zero bookings. Cheap leads are not wins until they book.

3 · Test designs

🎵 TikTok new ad group — SAR 400

Tiktok-Lead-Generation-SA-RUH-Female-All-Hydrafacial-Dermapen-Meso-20261108 · ~13/day
  • Why: Hydrafacial failed as a single-offer group (116 CPL). This tests a bundled skin offer with new creative.
  • Setup: Female, Riyadh, same instant form as Female-All-Laser. Two to three ads, one hook each.
  • Measure: CPL first, then CPB once 10 leads are in.
Gate on 15 Nov: kill at 0 leads after 150 SAR, or CPL above 60. Keep if CPB ≤ 250 after 10 leads.

👻 Snapchat gender split — SAR 300

…Femle-Laser-Offer-…-20261110 and …Male-Laser-Offer-…-20261110 · ~10/day
  • Why: women's offers book at ~190 and men's at 260–550. Separate squads let each get its own budget.
  • Setup: same campaign (All-Laser-Offer-20260620), one lead form per gender, so the CRM source now names the form.
  • Measure: CPB per squad against the All squad.
Gate on 15 Nov: if a split squad's CPB beats the All squad's, move up to 20% of the All squad's budget to it. Male squad: kill if CPB above 300 after 10 leads.

📸 Meta new ad sets — SAR 300

Instagram-LeadGeneration-SA-RUH-Female-IG-Posts-Laser/Hydrafacial-AUTHN-20261110 · ~10/day
  • Why: splitting the AUTHN ad set by offer shows which offer drives Meta bookings.
  • Setup: female, Riyadh, Instagram placements, lead form with one qualifying question (preferred branch/time) to raise lead quality.
  • Measure: booking rate, since Meta CPL is always cheap.
Gate on 22 Nov: kill an ad set at booking rate below 8% after 25 leads. Keep if CPB ≤ 220.

🗓 Weekday timing — no extra budget

TikTok and Snapchat core lines · 16–29 Nov
  • Why: Thu–Sat bring fewer leads at flat spend. The dashboard's Lead Timing panel shows Tuesday busiest and Sunday quietest so far in November.
  • Setup: +20% budget on the two strongest weekdays, −20% on the two weakest, same weekly total.
  • Measure: CPB by weekday against 1–15 Nov.
Read on 29 Nov: keep the schedule only if blended CPB falls at least 10%.

5 · Optimization rules & calendar

Always-on rules

  • Pause an ad group at 450 SAR spent with 0 bookings, or CPB above 285 (1.5× target) after 30 leads.
  • Refresh creative at frequency above 5, or CTR falling 7 days in a row.
  • Budget steps of ±20% at most, no more than one change per line per 3 days.
  • Scale a line only when its 7-day CPB is ≤ 190 and it has at least 5 bookings.
  • Platform cap: stop scaling a platform once its projected month-end spend reaches its cap (section 6).
  • Lead chase: any lead still "inquiry" after 48 hours gets a call and a WhatsApp.

Calendar (Sunday reviews)

  • 1–7 Nov · Fix delivery, run the base allocation, ramp Meta.
  • Sun 8 Nov · First weekly review: pacing per platform, kill obvious losers.
  • Sun 15 Nov · Mid-month gate · Test gates, reserve tranche 1 (1,250), October attendance mature.
  • Sun 22 Nov · Reserve tranche 2 (1,250), Meta test gate, move 20% steps from worst to best CPB line.
  • 23–30 Nov · White Friday · Concentrate on the best offer and line.
  • Sun 29 Nov · Weekday test read-out.
  • 30 Nov · Wrap-up: final CPB per line, December carry-over.

7 · Open decisions & data gaps

  • Releasing the reserve needs the owner's OK even when the gate passes.
  • Leads with no CRM source show as their own row, outside any platform's numbers.
  • Snapchat leads with an empty CRM source can't be tied to a squad: Snapchat no longer offers a lead list, only the form name in the source.
  • TikTok bookings are tied at ad group level only: TikTok lead lists don't name the ad.
  • Meta spend lags one day; Meta leads are matched from manual CSV exports.
  • Lead hour: some leads carry the time they were entered into the CRM, not when they arrived (a 15:00 batch on 11 Nov).
  • No revenue data, so break-even CPB is unknown. Average ticket would let the CPB limits be set from margin.
