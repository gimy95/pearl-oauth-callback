Expected leads by campaign and ad group

Expected leads = budget ÷ assumed cost per lead. Proven ad groups use their Sep–Oct platform CPL. The two unproven groups (TikTok Posts, Meta) use observed CPL × 1.4 for expected, × 2 for conservative, because cheap early leads usually get dearer when spend scales. Conservative for proven groups = CPL + 25%. Platform-reported leads: the CRM counts about 7% fewer for TikTok and about 19% fewer for Snapchat.

What this implies for bookings. At Sep–Oct booking rates (TikTok 17%, Snapchat 19%, Meta 10%), the base plan gives about 41 bookings from TikTok, 12 from Snapchat and 23 from Meta, roughly 76, plus about 12 if the reserve is released. That lands inside the 75–90 target, but about 30% of it rests on Meta leads booking at 10%, which is unproven.
Context: October produced 285 CRM leads on 9.9k at about 35 per lead. A blended 23 per lead in November only works if Meta keeps its cheap leads at scale. In the conservative case the blend is about 31 per lead.
Reserve gates. Release 1,250 on 15 Nov and 1,250 on 22 Nov (the White Friday push, 23–30 Nov). Each release needs all three, on a rolling 7 days:
  • Blended CPB 200 SAR or lower
  • Booking rate 15% or higher
  • No-show rate not rising
If a gate fails, the reserve stays unspent.

5. Targets and kill rules

  • Pause a platform or ad set if CPB is above 1.5× the account average (about 285) after at least 30 leads.
  • Pause an ad at frequency above 5, or CTR falling for 7 days.
  • Scale a winner by at most +20% per change.
  • Meta check: if 25 or more November leads show no attendance by 20 Nov, hold it at its 15 Nov level.
  • Weekday test: bias +20% Sun–Tue and −20% Thu–Sat on TikTok and Snapchat for two weeks, then compare CPB.

6. Not spend, but worth more than spend

  • Cut no-shows. WhatsApp reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before each appointment. Going from about 40% to 25% no-shows is worth as much as a 25% cut in CPB.
  • Answer leads within 5 minutes, with a three-touch follow-up over two days.

7. Gaps

  • TikTok and Meta bookings can't be tied to an ad group or ad. Fix: ingest the lead forms again (stale since May and July) or put the ad name in the CRM source field, as Snapchat already does.
